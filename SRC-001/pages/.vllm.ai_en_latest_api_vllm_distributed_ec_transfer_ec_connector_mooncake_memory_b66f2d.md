#

`vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory`

[¶](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory)

Registered-memory allocation and residency for Mooncake transfers.

The Producer pool stages source tensors in a registered slab. The Consumer pool owns destination allocations from reservation through publication, resident reuse, CUDA-safe retirement, and pressure-driven reclamation.

Classes:

-
–[ConsumerMemoryPool](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory.ConsumerMemoryPool)Own the registered receive slab and resident allocation lifecycle.

-
–[ContiguousAllocator](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory.ContiguousAllocator)Own a registered slab and its aligned regions; callers serialize access.

-
–[MemoryAllocation](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory.MemoryAllocation)Describe a tensor view carved from the Consumer receive slab.

-
–[ProducerMemoryPool](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory.ProducerMemoryPool)Own the Producer staging slab and regions carved from it.

-
–[ResidentLease](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory.ResidentLease)Represent a borrow of one resident entry.

-
–[ResidentPool](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory.ResidentPool)Track resident values, active leases, and LRU eviction eligibility.

-
–[StagedSources](https://docs.vllm.ai#vllm.distributed.ec_transfer.ec_connector.mooncake.memory.StagedSources)Own Producer tensor views and their staging-slab regions.
