why can’t i create any cpu and ZeroGPU spaces?

i’m on free tier, i should be able to create 2 ZeroGPU spaces. When i log into my old account where i have 2 ZeroGPU spaces (which are sleeping do i’m not abusing double accounting), it says i can only create 2 for free.

on my new account, i don’t have any ZeroGPU spaces and it then says i have to pay for Pro Tier to create ZeroGPU spaces.

is this a bug?

Matches a known account-age gate rather than a bug: free-tier ZeroGPU access (2 Spaces) requires the account to be at least 30 days old, that’s a newer restriction and it’s not documented clearly anywhere obvious. Your old account clears that bar since it already has the 2 Spaces; your new account almost certainly doesn’t yet, hence it’s pushing you to PRO instead of just letting you use the free ZeroGPU quota. If the new account is close to 30 days old, it should unlock on its own, if it’s much newer than that, that’s almost certainly the actual cause here.
