### Feature Description

A `--reasoning-budget` like argument that allows us to control the budget based on the conversation length. There could also be another argument that does the same, but based only on the current prompt.

Examples:

`--reasoning-budget 0.5`: the max reasoning tokens will be 50% of the conversation lenght;
`--reasoning-budget 0.5+4000`: the max reasoning tokens will be 50% of the conversation length plus 4000 tokens;
`--reasoning-budget 0.5-2300`: the max reasoning tokens will be 50% of the conversation length minus 2300 tokens;
`--reasoning-budget-current 0.3`: the max reasoning tokens will be 30% of the current prompt lenght;
`--reasoning-budget-current 0.3+4000`: the max reasoning tokens will be 30% of the current prompt length plus 4000 tokens;
`--reasoning-budget-current 0.3-2300`: the max reasoning tokens will be 30% of the current prompt length minus 2300 tokens

### Motivation

Having a fixed reasoning budget might not be good for all tasks. Imagine that you send a 50k prompt. Having a reasoning budget of only 4k could probably be not enough. If the reasoning budget is too low, it will be the same as not having reasoning at all in practice. If the reasoning budget is too high, it kinda defeats the purpose of having a reasoning budget.
