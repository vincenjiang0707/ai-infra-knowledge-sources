# Butter is joining Modal

Today we're excited to announce that [Butter](http://butter.dev/) is joining Modal!

Butter founder Erik Dunteman and researcher Raymond Tana will be joining the [Modal Sandbox](https://modal.com/docs/guide/sandboxes) team as part of the acquisition.

## Expanding Modal Sandboxes

The Butter team's experience is in agent harness engineering, with past focuses on [computer-use agents](https://pig.dev), [deterministic memory systems](https://github.com/pig-dot-dev/muscle-mem), skills, and codegen. Most recently, they created [bVisor](https://github.com/butter-dot-dev/bvisor), a virtual linux kernel built from scratch in Zig to solve their own need for lightweight, ephemeral sandboxes.

By bringing Butter's engineering and expertise to Modal, we're taking one step closer to making Modal Sandboxes a best-in-class product experience.

Modal's relationship with Erik (Dunteman) goes years back, starting from his time as cofounder of [Banana](https://banana.dev), a serverless GPU platform. What started as a friendly rivalry turned into mutual respect, with Erik briefly contracting with Modal before going on to start Butter. We are thrilled to welcome him back home.
