## 正文

Move Tutorial 06 multi-CTA attention out of #10812 and stack it above #10817. Enable opt-in 2/4/8-CTA forward launches on Blackwell, extend accumulator splitting, and autotune register limits with CUDA-graph timing. Keep one CTA by default, CTA-group-1 MMA, and no automatic TMA multicast.

Kernel-level search: 4,200 configurations (tiles, stages 1–5, 4/8 warps, ten register settings), plus 1,808 refinements across sequence lengths, D64/D128, and causal attention. Retain stages 2–4 and default/80/96/112/128/144/168 register settings; keep baseline configurations where proposed caps regress.

**TFLOPS:** GB200, FP16, B=4, H=32, N=4096, D=128, non-causal. Fixed compiled kernels, 32 launches per graph, 30-second sustained warmup, two runs of 30 rotating rounds; pooled median, host setup excluded.

| CTAs | WS off | Previous WS | Tuned WS | WS warps / register cap |
| --: | --: | --: | --: | -- |
| 1 | 948.8 | 1043.0 | 1039.2 | 4 / 168 |
| 2 | 884.7 | 1039.8 | 1039.8 | 4 / 168 |
| 4 | 897.5 | 528.4 | 868.9 | 8 / default (96 initial) |
| 8 | 892.7 | 434.8 | 732.4 | 8 / 96 |

4/8-CTA WS reported spill words drop from 84 to 8. 1/2-CTA WS performance is approximately unchanged; 4/8 CTAs still trail them.

Validation: 104 forward/gradient/FP8 tests and 4,532 numerical/IR/PTX candidate checks pass. ConSan passes 2-CTA causal/non-causal and 4-CTA non-causal with allocation poisoning disabled. Instrumented 4-CTA causal and 8-CTA compilation remain blocked by allocation-metadata and tensor-size errors; both have standalone reproducers. No ConSan changes included.
