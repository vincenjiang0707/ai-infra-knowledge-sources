## 正文

* [AMD][GFX1250] TDM copy reordering in the pipeliner

Summary

Adds a scheduling variant to the AMD stream pipeliner that reorders TDM-fed loop bodies for better async overlap. The new schedule issues all async_tdm_copy_global_to_local operations first, followed by an async_tdm_wait, then the local loads and dot -- improving latency hiding.

Motivation

The previous schedule issued async_tdm_wait first, then interleaved async_tdm_copy_global_to_local and local_load operations, followed by the dot. This had two key drawbacks:
- Global loads were issued after the waitcnt, draining the current copies before the next were issued and effectively serializing consecutive iterations' copies.
- There're explicit barriers between async_tdm_copy_global_to_local and local_load operations.

The new schedule addresses both issues: by issuing global loads before the waitcnt, the waitcnt latency can be hidden. This is especially beneficial in the memory-bound regime, where the current TDM copies may overlap with the next TDM copies on the TDM engine. Additionally, the barriers between async_tdm_copy_global_to_local and local_load are removed naturally. Note that removing the barriers alone only yields a 3-5% improvement; the majority of the gain comes from issuing global loads before waitcnt.

Performance

- The reordering yields a 10-29% improvement in Triton MXFP GEMM performance on GFX1250;
- After reordering, triton performance reaches 80-100% of Gluon performance on GFX1250.

Details

- The reordering is currently gated behind the TRITON_HIP_DISABLE_TDM_REORDER flag (off by default).
- Adds a lit test.
