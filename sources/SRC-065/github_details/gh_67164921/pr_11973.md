## 正文

SM12x targets do not support TCGen05, but TCGen05/TMEM operations could reach lowering and cause an LLVM abort when ConSan inserted a wait.

Reject these operations during NVIDIA GPU IR verification on unsupported targets. Update affected test targets and add compile-only coverage with and without ConSan.

Fixes #11747.
