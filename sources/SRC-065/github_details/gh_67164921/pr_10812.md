## 正文

Enable automatic warp specialization for two-CTA MMA, including TMA readiness, TMEM reuse, and aref teardown. Barrier selection follows producer/consumer synchronization; pure two-CTA MMA handoffs use local completion barriers. No automatic TMA multicast. Generic accumulator-lifetime fixes are in #11942.

Tutorial 09 on GB200 at [`108d1d4be6`](https://github.com/triton-lang/triton/commit/108d1d4be6e0366c1f5b54ae0e05f491c4a4db7b), FP16, M=N=8192. Tuned all 48 configurations per variant using warmed 32-launch CUDA graphs, excluding configurations that exceed hardware resources. TFLOPS below use separate measurements: two runs of 30 rotating rounds, 32 launches per graph after warmup, host setup excluded. cuBLASLt uses a 32 MiB workspace. Run medians differed by at most 4.3%.

| K | cuBLASLt | 1 CTA | 1 CTA + WS | 2 CTAs | 2 CTAs + WS |
| --: | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| 64 | 363.4 | 335.0 | 334.2 | 323.6 | 316.7 |
| 512 | 964.7 | 890.2 | 1060.9 | 959.4 | 1156.3 |
| 2048 | 1215.7 | 1168.9 | 1239.9 | 1207.2 | 1304.1 |
| 8192 | 1268.4 | 1183.1 | 1232.4 | 1268.6 | 1299.3 |

Validation: 9 lit tests, 39 numerical tests, and correctness/IR/PTX checks on all 592 runnable tuning candidates. Checks verify requested specialization, two-CTA MMA, and no TMA multicast. The barrier protocol also passed 18 focused ConSan checks across 2/4/8 CTAs with allocation poisoning enabled. Full matmul ConSan remains incomplete (prior reports/timeouts); `num_stages=0` has a known hoisting assertion.
