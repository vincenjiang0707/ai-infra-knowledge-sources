## 正文

Prefer M-first CTA ordering and legal M/N splits for Blackwell descriptor matmuls, enabling two-CTA MMA in 4/8-CTA clusters. Ports the layout policy from #10769 while preserving eligibility checks. No automatic TMA multicast or tutorial changes.

**TFLOPS (higher is better).** GB200, FP16 input/output, FP32 accumulation, M=N=8192. Tutorial 09 persistent TMA kernel; full 48-config tuning for 1/2 CTAs, extended tile/grid search for 4/8 CTAs. Selected configurations are fixed during measurement. 4/8-CTA WS uses `maxnreg=128` and grids of 36/15 clusters; 1/2 CTAs retain default registers. cuBLASLt uses the same inputs and a 32 MiB workspace.

32 launches per CUDA graph after warmup, 30 rotating rounds, two runs with reversed order. TFLOPS = 2MNK / mean of the two median times. These replace the earlier fixed-config M=N=4096 throughput table.

| 64 | 357.5 | 334.2 | 331.2 | 323.6 | 316.7 | 290.2 | 258.4 |
| 512 | 965.4 | 881.9 | 1039.1 | 937.3 | 1072.4 | 1059.2 | 1007.8 |
| 2048 | 1286.4 | 1231.3 | 1349.6 | 1316.0 | 1378.1 | 1278.8 | 1093.4 |
| 8192 | 1344.7 | 1201.2 | 1234.8 | 1289.0 | 1314.9 | 1262.9 | 1075.6 |

The register cap and grid sizes are benchmark settings, not compiler-default changes. Separate 4/8-piece epilogue experiments are not included in this PR.

Validation: benchmark numerics/IR/PTX pass; the table's kernels have zero spills. Compiler regression checks: 13 lit and 122 pytest pass. Four 4-CTA ConSan cases pass with allocation poisoning disabled; 8-CTA WS ConSan compilation timed out.
