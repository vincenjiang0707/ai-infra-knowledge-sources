# Coyote Lake Harvey Bear Ranch County Park

Introduction

On August 22nd, 2026, after attending the Race2Unravel 10K race at Gilroy, CA, I visited Coyote Lake Harvey Bear Ranch County Park for a short hike and photography. It has been four years since my last visit to this place. Last time, I remember I encountered lots of Mule Deers and even some Wild Pigs. This time, I also encountered them and took some photographs of Mule Deers. For wild pigs, they were too far away on the beach while I was in the car in the middle of the road, so I was not able to take any photographs of them.

Camera System Setup

- Camera: Sony Alpha 1 II
- Lens: Tamron 50-400mm F/4.5-6.3 Di III VC VXD
