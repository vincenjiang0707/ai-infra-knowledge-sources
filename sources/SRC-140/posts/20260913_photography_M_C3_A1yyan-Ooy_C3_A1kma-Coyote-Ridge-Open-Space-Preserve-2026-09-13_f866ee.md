# Máyyan Ooyákma - Coyote Ridge Open Space Preserve

Introduction

On September 13th, 2026, my friend and I visited Máyyan Ooyákma - Coyote Ridge Open Space Preserve for hiking. I brought my camera and took some photographs along the way. I was hoping to see some Tule Elks, which I missed seeing last time I visited here, but unfortunately, we did not encounter any during this trip. Nevertheless, we still encountered a Tarantula and an American Kestrel, and I was able to take a few photographs of them.

- Camera: Sony Alpha 1 II
- Lens: Sony FE 100-400mm F4.5 GM OSS
