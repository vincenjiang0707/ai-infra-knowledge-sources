## Purpose

Followup to https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/57250 - helps decoding for diffusiongemma in both text and structured answer mode (the latter only at high concurrency, the former in all cases I've found).

A custom diffusiongemma Triton kernel: 

 - one program per logits row
 - does an online max and sum in one pass
 - draws Gumbel noise inline from Philox instead of materializing a noise tensor
 - writes the softmax once in the embedding dtype (only when self-conditioning will read it)

Replaces _compiled_sample_step's older process which was several fused kernels that performed:

 - Cast the logits to fp32 and divide every element by the request's temperature.
 - Draw a uniform random tensor the same size as the logits, turn it into Gumbel noise with two logs, add it to the scaled logits, and take the argmax of that for the sample.
 - Take the argmax of the scaled logits themselves for the committed token.
 - Compute log_softmax over each row, exponentiate it to get the probabilities, multiply the two and sum for the entropy.
 - Cast the probabilities to bf16 for the self-conditioning matmul.

(each of those is at least one read and often one write of a [4096, 262144] fp32 tensor at concurrency 32)
