# [Issue #58228] [Bug]: finish_reason is "tool_calls" for a turn truncated at max_tokens, hiding the length stop from clients

source: https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/58228
state: open | updated: 2026-09-23T06:24:03Z
labels: tool-calling

## 正文

**Environment.** As in A.

**Describe the bug.** When a generation is cut off by `max_tokens` but any tool call was parsed, the response reports `finish_reason: "tool_calls"` rather than `"length"`. In `vllm/entrypoints/openai/chat_completion/serving.py` the finish reason is computed as `auto_tools_called or (tool_choice == "required" and output.finish_reason == "stop")`, and `auto_tools_called` wins regardless of why generation stopped.

**Impact.** A client cannot tell a completed tool-calling turn from one guillotined mid-argument. Agent harnesses use `finish_reason` to decide whether to continue, retry with a larger budget, or execute; here they execute a call that may be the last complete one before the cut. It also silently corrupts any analysis that filters on the finish reason.

**Frequency.** 25 of 3,600 generations in our suite hit the cap exactly; each reported `tool_calls`. They were identifiable only by comparing `usage.completion_tokens` with the request's `max_tokens`.

**Suggested fix.** Let `length` win over the tool-call relabel, or surface the underlying stop reason alongside it.

AI assistance was used for the investigation and the measurement harness; I reviewed the findings and ran everything described here on my own hardware.
