## 正文

## What?
Backport https://github.com/openucx/ucx/pull/11920 to 1.23

Raise UD_TIMEOUT from 1s to 3s in stale_private_ep_reuse and private_ep_keepalive.

## Why?
CI hit `ud_ep.c: Fatal: UD endpoint ... unhandled timeout error` in `stale_private_ep_reuse` on the ASAN stage. The failing job stalled for ~2s (other UD tests in it ran 2s slower than in every other ASAN job). With a 1s UD timeout, a stall while a CREQ is in flight makes the UD timer declare a peer timeout on a user endpoint before the already arrived CREP is processed. The UD gtest fixture has no error handler, so that is fatal and kills the whole shard. 3s matches `test_ud_peer_failure` and survives such stalls; the keepalive idle window grows accordingly.
