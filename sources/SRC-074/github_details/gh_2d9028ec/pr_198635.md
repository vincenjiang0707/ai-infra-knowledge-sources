## 正文

First reported here https://github.com/kornia/kornia/issues/4522
Adjacent dim reductions always ran general kernel which suffered bad perf. This PR improves it. Perf:

| op | shape | dims | f32 before (us) | f32 after (us) | f32 speedup | bf16 before (us) | bf16 after (us) | bf16 speedup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean | [32, 64, 56, 56] | (2, 3) | 722.7 | 74.2 | 9.7x | 708.7 | 31.1 | 22.8x |
| mean, channels_last | [32, 64, 56, 56] | (2, 3) | 1007.9 | 76.9 | 13.1x | 976.6 | 31.5 | 31.0x |
| var | [32, 64, 56, 56] | (2, 3) | 935.8 | 78.4 | 11.9x | 934.8 | 42.0 | 22.2x |
| sum | [4096, 64, 64] | (1, 2) | 1272.4 | 234.8 | 5.4x | 1263.7 | 112.4 | 11.2x |
| mean | [8, 512, 4096] | (0, 1) | 2000.7 | 265.9 | 7.5x | 1956.5 | 131.4 | 14.9x |
| amax | [16, 128, 128, 32] | (1, 2) | 889.5 | 131.5 | 6.8x | 900.8 | 46.6 | 19.3x |
| std | [8, 64, 64, 64] | (1, 2, 3) | 408.8 | 47.7 | 8.6x | 414.7 | 52.6 | 7.9x |
| mean | [8, 16, 32, 32] | (2, 3) | 27.9 | 4.5 | 6.2x | 27.5 | 3.6 | 7.6x |
