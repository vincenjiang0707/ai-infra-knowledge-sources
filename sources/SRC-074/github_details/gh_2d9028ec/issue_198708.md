# [Issue #198708] [MPS] torch.expm1 has only the precision of exp(x) - 1 for small x

## 正文

### 🐛 Describe the bug

> **Disclosure:** filed with AI assistance (Claude Code, at my request). The measurements were made on my MacBook Pro; the analysis below was produced by the assistant and is quoted as such — I am the human responsible for this report.

> On MPS, `torch.expm1` has the precision of `exp(x) - 1` for small `x`: the result equals `torch.exp(x) - 1` computed in float32, so the extra precision `expm1` exists for is lost. CPU is fine.
>
> ```python
> import torch
> x = torch.tensor(-0.0074433, dtype=torch.float32)
> print(torch.expm1(x).item())             # cpu: -0.0074160378  (float64 reference: -0.0074160377)
> print(torch.expm1(x.to("mps")).item())   # mps: -0.0074160099
> print((torch.exp(x) - 1).item())         # float32 exp(x)-1:   -0.0074160099  <- identical to the MPS result
> ```
>
> Relative error 2.3e-5 on MPS vs 4.7e-8 on CPU for this input. A quick survey over `expm1`, `log1p`, `exp`, `log`, `sqrt`, `pow`, `tanh` with the same inputs: only `expm1` is degraded (`log1p` is at 1.0e-7).
>
> Real-world impact: ComfyUI's UniPC sampler builds its step coefficients from `expm1(h)` through a recurrence that cancels for small `h`; with this error the third coefficient comes out as 55 instead of 0.25 and Wan 2.2 video diffusion renders garbage on Apple silicon (reported separately to ComfyUI with a fix on their side).
>
> Reproduced on torch 2.14.0 and 2.13.0 (pip wheels), Python 3.12.11, macOS 27.0 (26A428), MacBook Pro 14-inch, Apple M2 Max, 96 GB.
