# [PR #5358] fp8: drhs_grad_quantization_calibration_method (static range on the weight-gradient arm; removes the per-layer absmax MAX reduction over DCN)

## 正文

## Summary

Adds `drhs_grad_quantization_calibration_method`: a calibration override for the cotangent of the weight-gradient matmul only (qwix `drhs_grad`, dW = X^T dY) under `quantization=fp8_full`. `null` (default) inherits `bwd_quantization_calibration_method`, so existing configs are unchanged. The activation-gradient arm (dX = dY W^T) keeps its own calibration.

## Why

With `bwd_quantization_calibration_method=absmax`, qwix computes the fp8 scale for each backward matmul as a per-channel absmax over the contracting axis. For the weight-gradient arm that axis is the token axis, which data parallelism shards across FSDP, EP and, in multi-slice runs, across the DCN slices. Every layer therefore carries a MAX all-reduce of the scale vector across slices, and a MAX cannot join the SUM gradient bucket that XLA sinks over DCN. In a two-slice DeepSeek-V3 run this per-layer reduction was the largest remaining DCN cost after the gradient buckets were fixed, and it also carried most of the step-time jitter.

A static range on that arm removes the reduction with no compiler involvement. `fixed,0.01` keeps the loss trajectory identical (to three decimals at every step of a 25-step synthetic run at 2 x 512, and on the MLPerf DeepSeek-V3 recipe with real data, checkpoint restore and eval it reaches the eval target one step earlier than the absmax control). `fixed,0.1` was bracketed and loses a small amount of loss, so 0.01 is the recommended value for this model. The activation-gradient arm must keep absmax: a fixed range on both arms underflows the small dX values through the layer stack.

## Reproducing the two-slice DeepSeek-V3 result (2 x 8x8x8 tpu7x, DCN data parallelism 2)

Two slices with this flag and the three XLA DCN bucket flags match a single 8x8x16 slice of the same 1024 chips within 2% on step time, with an identical loss trajectory.

## Testing

- AOT compile (tpu7x-1024 x 2): the per-layer MAX megascale send disappears; DCN sends per step 14 -> 10; HLO temporaries unchanged.
- 2 x 8x8x8, DCN DP=2, 25 synthetic steps: loss identical to the absmax control at every step; step time within 2% of one 8x8x16 slice.
- MLPerf DeepSeek-V3 recipe on one 8x8x8 slice (real c4 data, checkpoint restore, eval): eval target reached one step earlier than the absmax control, with a lower eval loss at every eval step.
- Default (`null`) leaves the qwix rules unchanged.
