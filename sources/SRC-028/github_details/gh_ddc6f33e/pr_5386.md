## 正文

## Summary
- Change `jax.lax.Precision.HIGH` to `jax.lax.Precision.HIGHEST` in `_gdn_matmul` (`src/maxtext/kernels/gdn/gdn_bwd/pallas_mosaic_tpu_bwd.py`) when `precise=True` for `bfloat16` activations.
- **Why**: Pallas Mosaic TPU lowering for `jax.lax.Precision.HIGH` (`#tpu.contract_precision<bf16x3>`) was introduced in JAX `0.11.1`. On JAX `0.11.0`, `Precision.HIGH` raises `NotImplementedError: Unsupported dot precision: HIGH`, whereas `Precision.HIGHEST` (`#tpu.contract_precision<fp32>`, 6-pass `bf16x6`) is supported across both JAX `0.11.0` and `0.11.1+`.
- Also adds `Args:` and `Returns:` sections to the `_gdn_matmul` docstring to satisfy Google3 / pylint docstring checks.
