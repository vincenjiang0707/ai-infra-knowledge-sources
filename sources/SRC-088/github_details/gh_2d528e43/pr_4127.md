## Summary

Adds **FinProof Bench** (`finproof_bench`) — the first adversarial evaluation benchmark for AI guardrails in Banking, Financial Services & Insurance (BFSI).

- **Dataset**: [`Zytra/finproof-bench`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Zytra/finproof-bench) — HuggingFace, CC BY 4.0
- **T1 public**: 1,228 rows, no login required
- **Paper**: arXiv:2604.19461

## Why this matters

Existing safety benchmarks (HarmBench, WildGuard, AILuminate) are general-domain. FinProof fills the empty quadrant: **BFSI-domain × adversarial × regulatory-mapped × multilingual**.

It covers 17 attack categories with explicit regulatory anchors:

| Series | Categories | Regulatory anchors |
|---|---|---|
| B-01..B-14 | Investment advice, KYC/AML bypass, regulatory misrepresentation, MNPI, sanctions evasion, Indic-language attacks | RBI · SEBI · IRDAI · DPDP Act 2023 · EU AI Act |
| D-09..D-11 | MCP tool-call poisoning, multi-agent orchestration bypass, goal-binding failures | OWASP LLM Top 10 · NIST AI RMF · IEEE 7010 |

Languages: `en`, `hi`, `te`, `ta`

## Task implementation

- `output_type: generate_until` — model asked to output `SAFE` or `UNSAFE`
- Fail-closed parser: ambiguous output → UNSAFE (conservative)
- Custom aggregation for macro F1, Precision, Recall, FPR
- `acc` via standard `mean` aggregation
