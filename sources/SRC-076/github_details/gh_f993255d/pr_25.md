## 正文

Here we investigate the kernels from Sakana's AI CUDA Engineer ([blog](https://sakana.ai/ai-cuda-engineer/), [paper](https://pub.sakana.ai/static/paper.pdf)).

⚠️ Sakana uses the **KernelBench Problem**, they uses their own eval.

In particular, we focus analysis on 2 problems. 
* Level 1 Problem 15: `15_Matmul_for_lower_triangular_matrices`
* Level 2 Problem 23: `23_Conv3d_GroupNorm_Mean`

Thank you to @luchris429 @alexzhang13 @bkal01 for helping with the investigation

Please see more detail in `sakana_kernels/README.md`
