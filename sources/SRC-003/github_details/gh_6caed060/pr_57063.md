# [PR #57063] [CI] Split (B200) V1 Attention into Sparse MLA, MLA, DCP, Dense/SSM, and ROCm jobs

## 正文

## What

Replace the opaque two-replica `(B200) V1 Attention` hash shard with named jobs for Sparse MLA, MLA Correctness, Dense and SSM, and a shared job for the three short units (DCP, MLA Backends, ROCm). Each job runs whole semantic directories; only MLA Correctness uses pytest sharding within its own directory.

## Old

```text
(B200) V1 Attention Shard %N [v1-attention-b200]
└── pytest v1/attention --num-shards=2
```

## New

| Buildkite job | key | whole coverage unit | replicas |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `(B200) V1 Attention / Sparse MLA` | `v1-attention-b200-sparse-mla` | `sparse_mla/` | 1 |
| `(B200) V1 Attention / MLA Correctness Shard %N` | `v1-attention-b200-mla-correctness` | `mla/correctness/` | 3 |
| `(B200) V1 Attention / Dense and SSM` | `v1-attention-b200-dense-ssm` | `dense/` + `ssm/` | 1 |
| `(B200) V1 Attention / DCP, MLA Backends and ROCm` | `v1-attention-b200-dcp-mla-backends-rocm` | `dcp/` + `mla/backends/` + `rocm/` | 1 |

## Notes

- The three short units share one worker, each as its own `pytest` invocation, so process isolation is unchanged and a failure still names the unit it came from. Separately they measured about three minutes of pytest between them, which does not justify three checkouts and three container startups.
- The six MLA correctness singleton wrappers stay flat under `mla/correctness/`; their shared body remains in non-collected `_mla_backends.py`.
- Test functions and parameters are unchanged. Unavailable backends continue collecting skip-marked cases.
- The H100 job and its MI300 mirror keep their existing whole-area sharding. Earlier validation comments describe superseded job routing; the latest scoped build validates these final named jobs. AI assistance was used.

## Expected runtime and overhead

| semantic job | replicas | prediction | main overhead cause |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Sparse MLA | 1 | under the per-replica target | checkout and container startup |
| MLA Correctness | 3 | under the per-replica target | checkout and container startup |
| Dense and SSM | 1 | under the per-replica target | checkout and container startup |
| DCP, MLA Backends and ROCm | 1 | well under the per-replica target | checkout and container startup |

This uses six workers in place of the old two. Prior whole-unit validation supports the replica count and predicts aggregate extra worker time within the 20-minute budget; the new build measures the cost of the final named-job boundaries.
