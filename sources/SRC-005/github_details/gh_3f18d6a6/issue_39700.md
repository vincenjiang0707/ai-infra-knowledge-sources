### Motivation

Here we record the roadmap of our team regarding the support for the PD separation-related functions of deepseekv4.1 on the B200 platform.
The functions that are expected to be developed for the Prefill node include:
- DSV4.1-PD-1/N-Prefill-CP  @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40323
- DSV4.1-PD-2/N-Prefill-CP-BCG @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40333
- DSV4.1-PD-3/N-Prefill-opt-stream-sync @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40335
- DSV4.1-PD-4/N-Prefill-cp-multistream-prepare @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40179
- DSV4.1-PD-7/N-Prefill-reuse kvcache @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40421
- DSV4.1-PD-8/N-Prefill-fuse candidate selection and filtering @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40436
- DSV4.1-PD-9/N-Prefill-Support indexer & compressor to be captured into BCG
The functions that the Decode node is expected to develop include:
- DSV4.1-PD-5/N-Decode-DP-DP_Attn @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40177
- DSV4.1-PD-6/N-Decode-Megamoe @https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/40178
