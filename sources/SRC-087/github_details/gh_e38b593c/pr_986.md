### speculatorsbot · 2026-08-15 · COMMENTED

Design is sound. When DFlash training reconstructs verifier logits from hidden states via `verifier_norm` + `verifier_lm_head`, it skips the post-lm_head transforms (`output_multiplier`, `final_logit_softcapping`) that the verifier's own forward pass applies. Adding `_transform_verifier_logits` at the target reconstruction site in `_backbone_forward` is the right fix for training parity with verifiers like [Muse Glimmer](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B) (where `output_multiplier=0.196`, `final_logit_softcapping=20.0`). The adapter for normalizing the released [Muse Glimmer assistant config](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B-assistant) (`model_type: "muse_glimmer_assistant"` to `qwen3`) is clean and appropriately scoped.

Backward compatibility is preserved through safe field defaults (`target_logit_scale=1.0`, `target_logit_softcap=None`). The `gt=0.0` constraint on `target_logit_softcap` already guards against the division-by-zero that the bot reviewers flagged. The verifier-config centralization is a good DRY cleanup.
