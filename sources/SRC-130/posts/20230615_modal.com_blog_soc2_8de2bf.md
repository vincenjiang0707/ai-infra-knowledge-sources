# Modal is SOC2 compliant

*Jan 2, 2025 update: Modal is now SOC 2 Type II compliant. Read the announcement here.*

## Industry-Standard Accreditation

At Modal, we’re committed to providing our customers with the highest level of security and trust. As part of that commitment, we’re proud to announce that we have successfully completed our SOC 2 Type I audit.

The SOC 2 audit is one of the highest recognized standards of information security compliance in the world. It was developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA) to allow a third-party auditor to validate a service company’s internal controls with respect to information security.

In order to prepare our SOC 2 Audit Report, a third-party auditor reviewed our internal controls including but not limited to data security, network configuration, change management, access control, backup and disaster recovery and security incident response plans.

SOC 2 is just one aspect of our growing security program. We are committed to continually improving our information security program and retaining an annual SOC 2 audit to ensure we keep supporting our customers’ needs.

You can request the report from our [Trust Center](https://trust.modal.com),
and read more about our security program [here](https://modal.com/docs/guide/security).
