## Empowering 1 billion developers

GitHub aims to accelerate human progress through developer collaboration and innovation and create one billion software developers. We believe the open source economy is central to this and is poised for growth.

### Fueling open source growth

GitHub Fund is partnering with M12 to provide the capital, connections, access, and benefits to help open source companies grow. We’re thrilled to partner with and invest in the next generation of open source entrepreneurs.

### Powering AI innovation

The open source software movement has brought rapid change to the developer community through the free exchange of ideas among distributed and diverse teams of developers. Open source is powering advancements in AI and leading the way.

## Investment themes

We invest in pre-seed to seed stage paradigm-changing companies that will transform the open source economy.

Technology that accelerates human productivity and progress, responsibly and ethically

Enabling unparalleled collaboration at scale and speed, globally

Cloud and data architecture that delivers secure, scalable performance for modern teams

Investing in cutting-edge security standards

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How much does GitHub Fund invest?


GitHub Fund, in partnership with M12—Microsoft’s venture capital fund—started with a $10 million commitment to invest in eight to ten companies each year.

#### What projects qualify for the GitHub Fund?


Our investment focus will be on open source developer tools in their early stages. We also focus on AI, security, infrastructure, and DevOps and are excited by the generative AI, LLM, AI-powered developer tools, and security trends we’re seeing.

#### What stage does GitHub Fund typically invest in?


We invest in pre-seed and seed stages. By partnering with companies early, we can support founders as the roots of their community form and their products take shape.

#### Is the investment dilutive?


It is an equity investment. We typically do not lead investment rounds. For non-dilutive funding, please check out GitHub Accelerator or GitHub Sponsors.
