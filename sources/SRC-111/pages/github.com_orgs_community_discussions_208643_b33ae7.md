Looks like this isn't a bug or a problem on your friend's end. Going by what GitHub Support told them, GitHub has paused new sign-ups with Outlook and Hotmail addresses because of ongoing fraud and abuse tied to those domains. That explains why the error shows up right on the signup form, before any verification email gets sent. It also explains why switching browsers, clearing cache, or checking spam didn't help.
