# Balancing Innovation with Governance: A Roadmap for AI-Powered Government

September 2, 2025

[Get the ebook](https://github.com#ebook-form)

This whitepaper provides a clear roadmap for navigating this new landscape, showing how GitHub’s AI-powered platform can empower your teams and strengthen governance.

The rise of AI is transforming how software is built, presenting a critical challenge for government agencies: how can you modernise development practices while maintaining the security, consistency, and compliance that public sector operations demand.

Read this whitepaper to discover how to:

**Strengthen Security**: Use fine-grained access controls, secret scanning, and automated policy enforcement to protect your entire workflow.**Ensure Consistency**: Drive quality across all projects with AI-assisted governance and automation.**Accelerate Collaboration**: Streamline developer workflows and onboarding with GitHub Copilot and cloud-ready Codespaces environments.**Overcome Challenges**: Get a clear roadmap for modernising legacy systems and a breakdown of common challenges in government IT.
