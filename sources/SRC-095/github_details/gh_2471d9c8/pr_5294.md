## Why are these changes needed?

Batch schedulers are becoming increasingly capable and scheduling requirements are being more complex and yet there is limited observability into the status of RayClusterss that rely on them to schedule workloads. Adding the ability for batch scheduler plugins to emit events and set status conditions on the RayCluster would greatly improve observability and debuggability for these clusters.

### kevin85421 · 2026-09-23

For the status conditions, have you investigated how other controllers (e.g. spark operator, jobset controller, ... etc) expose them? I’d be interested in learning how they handle this.

Also, to avoid this kind of back-and-forth in the future, perhaps we could discuss proposals like this during the community sync and make sure we’re aligned before implementation begins.

I would consider this out of scope because observability is typically provided by third-party schedulers, each of which usually has its own intermediate representation for expressing scheduling status. Take Kueue, for example: its observability relies entirely on its `Workload` CRs—Kueue’s intermediate representation—and users rely on these CRs for all related information, such as why a workload was not admitted, who preempted it, and how many times it was evicted.

### kevin85421 · 2026-09-22 · CHANGES_REQUESTED

It seems that the event recorder isn’t used anywhere.

I would suggest opening a separate PR for events first. In KubeRay, we use events to record write operations performed against the Kubernetes API server, such as creating or deleting Kubernetes resources.

As for status conditions, I think they may require further discussion, as they seem somewhat outside the scope of KubeRay.
