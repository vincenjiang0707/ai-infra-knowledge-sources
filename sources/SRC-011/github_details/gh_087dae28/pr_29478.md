**Why adding a barrier on FATTN Q4_0**:

Without the barrier, on GCN arch, there is VGPR spill due to LLVM scheduling issue. 
For Q4_0 FlashAttention V dequantization, keep the VMEM read close to its
consumer with an AMD scheduler group barrier. This avoids the VGPR pressure
regression caused by the optimized subtraction while improving occupancy on
the affected gfx906/gfx1152 kernels.
