## Overview

- Skip DMMV lanes past `ncols` before reading quantized weights and activations, fixing the 96-column row-tail read.
- Test shared-weight decode, batch, and decode reuse.

Tested and reviewed: 2026-09-23

## Results

Intel Arc Pro B70, oneAPI 2026.1.1. Baseline -> patch; SYCL optimizations disabled.

- Reuse: 16/16 cases pass in four runs. The unpatched control fails the 96-column case (15/16).
- Quantized `MUL_MAT_ID`: 239/241 pass. The two high-amplitude NaN cases also fail on pristine upstream.
- Aligned-row DMMV (`m=4096, k=14336, n=1`): 432.19 -> 434.63 us (+0.56% latency); baseline runs span 430.10-434.28 us.
