## Summary

Harden persistent GMS KV failover for vLLM and SGLang after their engine-specific MVPs. This train keeps inference-engine scheduling, hashes, cache indexes, and eviction policy native to each engine. GMS owns daemon-lifetime GPU allocations plus generation-fenced recovery metadata.

All implementation PRs remain drafts. On 2026-09-22 the existing PRs were updated in place, preserving their discussion history, and the complete stack was rebased onto Dynamo main at `2e4998a30ccd6298d81c4263a49b6fa9a0b273c6`.

Parent engine trackers: #12053 (vLLM) and #14704 (SGLang). Recovery-strategy discussion: #14828.

## Architecture

```text
engine-native allocator and prefix index
              |
engine adapter: transactional publish/adopt
              |
generation-fenced content directory
              |
block validity, TP agreement, writer retirement
              |
daemon-owned persistent GPU allocation (v0 or v1)
```

There is one serving owner. A replacement may reuse exact-generation sealed KV after writer retirement and safe classification. Ambiguous predecessor accesses remain quarantined unless a separately qualified GPU-quiescence provider proves they can be reclaimed. Process death, heartbeats, traffic silence, and fixed delays are not treated as CUDA-quiescence proof.

## Safety boundary

The earlier vLLM and SGLang checkpoints demonstrate the engine integrations and controlled crash loop. The complete arbitrary-crash safety model is cumulative through this hardening train. Reviewers should not infer final quarantine or writer-retirement guarantees from an earlier engine-only checkpoint.

Required invariants:

- Never serve a directory record whose slot generation is stale.
- Never admit a replacement writer before predecessor writers are retired and predecessor KV is classified.
- Require TP ranks to agree before allocation, adoption, reclamation, or takeover succeeds.
- Treat malformed, missing, reordered, or incompatible recovery metadata as a miss or startup failure.
- Keep directory and TP coordination off the per-token hot path.
- Preserve engine-native capacity, free-list, refcount, and rollback semantics.

## Scope

Included: local persistent HBM, vLLM/SGLang integration, TP recovery coordination, request continuity, v0/v1 pool backends, and failover hardening.

Excluded: KVCR tiering, host/storage tiers, remote transfer, router-directed P2P, decode-to-decode transfer, TensorRT-LLM, node/GPU-reset durability, and authenticated same-user multi-tenancy.
