# [Issue #15071] Make LPU eviction cleanup durable after trigger Pod deletion

state: closed | updated: 2026-09-23T15:03:47Z

## 正文

## Summary

Make node-local LPU eviction cleanup durable when the disrupted trigger Pod is deleted before the eviction reconciler processes it.

## Rationale

The current eviction flow enqueues a deleting trigger Pod. If Kubernetes deletes that Pod before reconciliation, `Reconcile` treats the missing Pod as completed. Matching sibling Pods can remain active. This can leave the LPU workload without coordinated replacement during disruption.

## Affected area

- `deploy/operator/internal/controller/lpx/eviction.go`
- LPU eviction watch, predicate, reconciliation, and sibling cleanup logic

## Required changes

Use a durable owner or cohort trigger that survives trigger-Pod deletion. For example, reconcile through the owning `PodClique` or another durable cohort identity. Do not mark cleanup complete when the trigger Pod is missing until the matching cohort has been processed.

Preserve the existing safety behavior:
- Delete only matching sibling Pods.
- Preserve native replacements.
- Keep ownership, UID, resource-version, template-revision, and runtime-partition validation.

## Acceptance criteria

- A disrupted LPU trigger Pod can disappear before the queued reconciliation runs.
- The controller still identifies and cleans up eligible sibling Pods.
- Native replacements and unrelated Pods remain preserved.
- Tests cover deletion of the trigger Pod before reconciliation.
