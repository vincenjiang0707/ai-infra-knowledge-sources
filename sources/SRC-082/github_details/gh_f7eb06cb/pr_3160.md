## 正文

## Summary

- Replace BitsAndBytes dense FP16 expansion with packed MLX inference.
- Execute NF4 and FP4 directly from packed nibbles with their exact checkpoint codebook and block scales in a tiled Metal kernel.
- Decode compressed 4-bit statistics once while retaining packed weights.
- Repack regular INT8 rows exactly into MLX native 8-bit affine layout; retain a packed Metal fallback for irregular widths.
- Preserve FP16/BF16 activation output dtype.
- Keep `BitsAndBytesMlxQuantLinear` as the validated GPT-QModel holder for platform, bit, shape, and source compatibility checks.

## Supported BitsAndBytes variants

| Bits | Format | Block sizes | Statistics | Runtime |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | NF4 | 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096 | plain or compressed | packed Metal codebook matmul |
| 4 | FP4 | 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096 | plain or compressed | packed Metal codebook matmul |
| 8 | INT8 | row scale | n/a | MLX 8-bit affine matmul; packed fallback for widths not divisible by 32 |
