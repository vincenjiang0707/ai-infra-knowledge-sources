## 正文

## Summary

Store MLX BitsAndBytes NF4/FP4 results directly in the activation dtype for small-output projections, avoiding the separate FP32-to-FP16/BF16 cast dispatch. The optimized region is deliberately limited to outputs with at most 2,048 channels, where every changed Qwen3.8-27B K/V case improved.

## What Changed

- Parameterized the packed 4-bit Metal kernel for FP16, BF16, and FP32 output storage.
- Enabled direct FP16/BF16 stores only for outputs with at most 2,048 channels.
- Kept larger 4-bit projections and the INT8 path on their existing implementation.
- Added exact regression checks against main's FP32-then-cast result.
- Extended Torch-oracle coverage to decode and 16-token prefill over every Qwen3.8-27B projection shape.
- Reworked the benchmark to cover NF4 and FP4, FP16 and BF16, decode and prefill, and all nine projection shapes.

No public API changes. Wider-output direct stores are intentionally out of scope because they did not show a stable end-to-end win.
