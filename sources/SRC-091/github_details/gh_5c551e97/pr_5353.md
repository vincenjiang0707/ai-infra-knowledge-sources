## 正文

**What this PR does / why we need it**:

Follow-up clean-ups after prefetch controller v2 (#5346). Three pieces of the pre-v2 design were left behind and are removed here:

- **Legacy prefetch types.** `TrimPolicy`, `PrefetchMode` and `PrefetchRequestSpec` in `internal_api.py` were only referenced by their trace codecs, `bitmap_ops.select_retained` and the `fold_unfold_ranked` / `fold_unfold` compositions. All of them are removed. The flat `fold` / `unfold` primitives stay as the test oracle for the grouped kernels; the microbenchmark composes them directly.
- **Query-lookup-hit path.** v2 reports the hit once, at finish, so the separate lookup-hit report is dead: `L2_PREFETCH_LOOKUP_COMPLETED`, the `lmcache_mp.l2_prefetch_hit` counter and its log handler, the deprecated `StorageManager.query_prefetch_lookup_hits`, the `LookupModule` handler and the `query_prefetch_lookup_hits` RPC (ZMQ legacy op 11 retired and reserved, gRPC messages dropped from `lookup_service.proto`). Nothing in `lmcache/integration/` used it.
- **Design docs.** `storage_manager.md` and the PrefetchController sections of `l2_adapters/overall.md` described the L1-first flow; they are rewritten for v2. `prefetch_l1_lock_pass.md` is replaced by `storage_controllers/prefetch_controller.md` (request grid, lock maps, invariants, per-step lock table, known limitations). The removed event and metric are dropped from the observability docs and the example Grafana dashboard.

**Special notes for your reviewers**:

- User-facing: the Prometheus counter `lmcache_mp_l2_prefetch_hit_chunks_total` no longer exists. The per-tier split is already available from `lmcache_mp_lookup_hit_{l1,l2}_tokens_total`, which `DEBUG.md` recommended over the removed counter.
- Events traces recorded before v2 that contain a `PrefetchRequestSpec` will no longer decode in the replay tool.
- `test_query_lookup_hits.py` also held three `lookup()` key-layout tests unrelated to lookup hits; they move unchanged to `tests/v1/multiprocess/test_lookup_key_groups.py`.
- Verified with the v2 handoff test list plus the transport, codec, bitmap-ops and metrics suites (1172 passed, including the gRPC transport parametrizations after regenerating the protos) and `pre-commit` on the changed files.
