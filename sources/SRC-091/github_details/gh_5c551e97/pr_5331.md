## 正文

Fixes #5187

## What changed

Converted lazy logging in the three files covered by this slice of #5118 to `%-format` and dropped their temporary `G004` ignores:

- `lmcache/observability.py`: `LMCacheStatsLogger.shutdown()` — `logger.info("Waiting for stats logger thread to finish (timeout: %ss)...", timeout)` and `logger.warning("Stats logger thread did not terminate within %ss timeout. ...", timeout)`.
- `lmcache/sdk/context.py`: `LMCacheSDKContext.__init__` — `logger.info("Initialized LMCacheSDKContext with instance_id=%s, model_name=%s, chunk_size=%s, shm_name=%s, kind=%s", ...)`.
- `lmcache/utils.py`: `handle_thread_exception()` — `logger.error("Thread %s crashed: %s: %s", args.thread.name, args.exc_type.__name__, args.exc_value)`.
- `pyproject.toml`: removed the `"lmcache/observability.py"`, `"lmcache/sdk/context.py"`, and `"lmcache/utils.py"` entries from `tool.ruff.lint.per-file-ignores`.

Rendered log text is unchanged in every case (`%ss` preserves e.g. `5.0s`, `%s` preserves the SDK context fields and thread crash triplet). No non-logging f-strings or control flow were touched.

## Why

This is one slice of the `G004` cleanup queue tracked in #5118 / #5125. These three top-level utility modules were still building log messages with f-strings, which formats eagerly even when the record is filtered out. Lazy `%`-style args defer that cost, and removing the ignores keeps the newly-clean files protected from regressing.

Refs #5118
Refs #3372

The change is logging-only with no behavior change beyond deferred formatting.
