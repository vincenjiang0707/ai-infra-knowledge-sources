## 正文

**What this PR does / why we need it**:

Adds a community-maintained FAQ at `docs/source/community/faq.rst`, published at
`docs.lmcache.ai/community/faq.html` and `docs.lmcache.ai/zh_CN/community/faq.html`,
and links it from the Community section and the README.

The content comes from an FAQ that Chinese-speaking community members have been
maintaining outside the repo, collecting questions from Slack, GitHub and
Xiaohongshu along with the answers maintainers gave. Bringing it into `docs/`
means it builds into the site, gets reviewed like the rest of the docs, and stops
drifting from the project.

**Special notes for your reviewers**:

*On the translation direction.* `docs/source/locale/README.md` establishes that
English `.rst` is the source of truth and Chinese lives in `.po` catalogs. This
content originated in Chinese, so following that rule naively would mean
machine-translating maintainers' own words back into Chinese. Instead the English
page is the gettext source as the convention requires, but `community/faq.po` is
filled with the community's **original Chinese wording** rather than a round-trip
translation. Every entry is complete and non-fuzzy, so the weekly
`translate_doc_zh.yml` run will not overwrite it.

The English text was written against the glossary in `tools/translate_docs_zh.py`
so terminology matches the rest of the docs.

*Three answers need maintainer confirmation* and are marked inline with a note.
They were incomplete or undated in the source FAQ, and I did not want to publish
them as settled:

| Question | What is missing | Suggested owner |
|---|---|---|
| Performance gains from enabling GDS | Discussion pointed at the uGDS paper but recorded no figures or citation | @sammshen @andyluo7 @ApostaC @hlin99 |
| CacheBlend open-source timeline | Original answer said "this quarter" with no date, so it cannot be relied on | @ApostaC @YaoJiayi |
| Does CacheBlend support linear attention models | No answer recorded, only an unidentified Xiaohongshu article | @ApostaC @YaoJiayi |

Answers are attributed to @maobaolong and @ApostaC, who gave them in the original
discussions. Please correct anything I have misattributed or misstated. Two edits
worth calling out: I dropped the internal triage fields (首次提问 / 重复提问次数)
and a session-scoped Xiaohongshu `xsec_token` URL that would rot, and I corrected
an apparent typo in the Chinese (重新过 → 重写过).
