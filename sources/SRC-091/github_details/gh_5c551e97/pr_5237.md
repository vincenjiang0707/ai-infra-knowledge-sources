## 正文

Fixes #5188

**What this PR does / why we need it**:

Converts the 17 `G004` findings across `lmcache/v1/api_server/__main__.py` (5), `lmcache/v1/server/__main__.py` (7) and `lmcache/v1/standalone/__main__.py` (5) to lazy `%`-style logging arguments, and removes the three matching `per-file-ignores` entries from `pyproject.toml`. One slice of the #5118 cleanup queue opened by #5125.

**Special notes for your reviewers**:

Rendered messages are unchanged. The two `exc_info=True` calls keep that keyword after the positional arguments.

Two nearby f-strings are deliberately untouched because they are not logging calls: the `ports_message` assignment in `api_server/__main__.py`, and the `ErrorMsg(...)` and `RuntimeError(...)` constructions.

Verification, per the issue:

```
ruff check --isolated --select G004 lmcache/v1/api_server/__main__.py lmcache/v1/server/__main__.py lmcache/v1/standalone/__main__.py
ruff check --select G004 lmcache/v1/api_server/__main__.py lmcache/v1/server/__main__.py lmcache/v1/standalone/__main__.py pyproject.toml
```

Both clean, plus `ruff check`, `ruff format --check` and `isort --check-only` on the touched paths with the pinned versions.
