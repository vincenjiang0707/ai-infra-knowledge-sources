# MiniMax-Hackathon

## 2025 Hackathon Winners 
### Travabelle
It’s a travel app that creates personalized walking tours, adapting to your schedule, interests, and language so you can explore any city freely without relying on rigid guided tours.
We designed the experience in Figma, then brought it to life using Lovable for app development. Supabase powers our backend, while Apify gathers up-to-date city data. For AI, we integrated Anthropic and **MiniMax TTS model** to personalize tours in real time—making each walk unique to the traveler.
