## 正文

PACE is a parallelism-aware collective engine for LLM training and inference. It fuses the memory operations that frameworks otherwise insert around communication — layout conversion, dtype casting, scatter/gather — directly into the collective kernels, and exposes a parallelism interface rather than the traditional collective interface: a compute kernel's output feeds straight into PACE, and PACE's output is consumed directly by the next kernel.
