# [PR #20371] [IR][Arith] Use BigInt-backed integer immediates and wide coefficients

source: https://github.com/apache/tvm/pull/20371
state: closed | updated: 2026-09-17T14:41:21Z
labels: 

## 正文

Store shared integer immediate values in the existing `ffi::BigInt`, preserving declared signedness and width checks. Full-range uint64 constants become ordinary `IntImm` nodes, and the `large_uint_imm` builtin is removed. Constant construction and folding use shared primitive helpers. Construction, transport, printing, and backend emission preserve the exact value or reject unsupported values and types.

Constant predicates recognize every IntImm, while consumers with native integer domains perform their own checked conversions. Immediate patterns capture the original typed node, with exact BigInt conditions and expression intermediates. Canonical and product coefficients retain exact values. Canonical split factors and their divisor/modulus arithmetic use BigInt, with an optional upper factor distinguishing finite moduli from no modulus. Signed rewrites preserve rounding, and IntImm construction enforces the declared integer range for synthesized constants. Typed folding preserves integer overflow contracts. Modular analysis and constant bounds keep their int64 domains and conservatively handle wider immediates. Canonical index-type eligibility, dimensions, counters and backend interfaces retain their existing limits.
