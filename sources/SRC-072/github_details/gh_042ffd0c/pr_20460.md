## 正文

Buffer signatures now carry function-input shape, dtype, layout, and offset metadata directly. Remove handle-based `match_buffer` declarations and their PrimFunc parameter-substitution machinery, and migrate builders, consumers, and documentation to `Buffer` annotations.

Keep buffer-subregion matching in the S-TIR namespace as `Ts.match_buffer`. Preserve forward-dependent parameter identity and avoid name capture when printing signatures alongside hoisted symbolic dimensions.
