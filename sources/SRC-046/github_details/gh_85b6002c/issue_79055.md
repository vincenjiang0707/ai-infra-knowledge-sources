### EntrigesFichtenholz · 2026-07-15

@morozovsevolod Thanks a lot, you pointed me in the right direction. (At least its not a paperweight.)

For future people finding this: AM4 boards seem to have a really hard time bringing up the PCIe link to this card. I tried a B350 and a B550 (Ryzen), in both the CPU-direct and chipset slots, with and without bifurcation, and forcing the link speed down (Gen1/Gen3), the card never showed up in lspci at all. Aux power connected, Above 4G on, cold boots, all the usual stuff. Nothing. The moment I moved it to an old AMD Athlon64 board (native PCIe 1.0), it enumerated instantly.

So if your K200 powers on (warm, LEDs lit) but stays invisible on a modern board: the card is almost certainly fine,  it's a PCIe link-training problem. The fix is a host that runs the link at a low generation. A native PCIe 1.0/2.0 board, or one that can genuinely force the card's slot down to Gen1/Gen2, does the trick.

Hope this saves someone the two days it cost me. :)

Edit: Maybe the new consumer boards are just too sloppy or fast thus the card cannot Enumerate propperly. I will try it with Server hardware in a couple of days.
