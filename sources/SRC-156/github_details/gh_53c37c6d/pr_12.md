## H200 validation on vLLM v0.27.1

The explicit 2 MiB policy was also evaluated in an earlier vLLM v0.27.1
campaign using:

- DeepSeek-V4-Pro
- one node with 8×NVIDIA H200
- TP8 + EP
- FlashInfer MNNVL
- BF16 all-reduce payloads
- FP8 KV cache
- speculative decoding disabled

```
# first
export VLLM_ALLREDUCE_USE_FLASHINFER=1
export VLLM_FLASHINFER_ALLREDUCE_BACKEND=mnnvl
# then add fi_allreduce_fusion_max_size_mb
--compilation-config  "pass_config":{"fi_allreduce_fusion_max_size_mb":2}
```

### Operator results

For payloads within 2 MiB, FlashInfer MNNVL had lower p50 latency than the symmetric-memory fallback:

| Payload | SymmMem p50 | FlashInfer MNNVL p50 | FI latency reduction |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.492 MiB | 11.130 us | 5.885 us | 47.12% |
| 0.998 MiB | 14.781 us | 8.554 us | 42.13% |
| 1.504 MiB | 16.458 us | 11.408 us | 30.68% |
| 1.982 MiB | 19.606 us | 13.760 us | 29.82% |
| 1.996 MiB | 18.653 us | 13.898 us | 25.49% |

The actual `CudaCommunicator.all_reduce` route was also verified:

| Tokens | Payload bytes | Observed route |
|---:|---:|---|
| 146 | 2,093,056 | FlashInfer MNNVL |
| 147 | 2,107,392 | symmetric-memory fallback |

Nsight Systems captured both routing paths:

- FlashInfer MNNVL: `twoshotAllreduceKernel`
- symmetric memory: `multimem_all_reduce_kernel`

### End-to-end results

The v0.27.1 model A/B used identical workloads and service settings for both arms. The only runtime difference was whether standalone FlashInfer all-reduce was enabled with the explicit 2 MiB limit.

The decode-heavy workload used an exact 1,024-token prompt, 4,096 generated tokens per request, two warmup waves per service block, and an A1 → B1 → B2 → A2 execution order.

| Workload | Baseline | Candidate | Paired median throughput gain | Positive pairs | Bootstrap 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1024→4096, concurrency 16 | 818.582 tok/s | 867.372 tok/s | +5.624% | 6/6 | [+4.628%, +7.659%] |
| 1024→4096, concurrency 32 | 814.355 tok/s | 857.659 tok/s | +5.315% | 6/6 | [+4.133%, +6.521%] |
