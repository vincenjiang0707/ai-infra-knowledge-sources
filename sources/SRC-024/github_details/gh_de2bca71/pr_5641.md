## 正文

## Motivation

The gfx950 rows for `token = 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384, 32768` of the DeepSeek-R1/V3 FP8 fused-MoE shape in
`aiter/configs/model_configs/a8w8_blockscale_tuned_fmoe_ds_v3.csv` all carry `us=359.4797` with a `block_m`=32 kernel. These seem to be copied from the `token=1024` measurement after #3024.

The affected shape is DeepSeek-R1/V3 at TP8 with shared expert fusion:

`model_dim`: 7168
`inter_dim`: 256
`expert`: 257
`topk`: 9
`act_type`: ActivationType.Silu
`dtype`: torch.bfloat16
`q_dtype_a`: torch.float8_e4m3fn
`q_dtype_w`: torch.float8_e4m3fn
`q_type`: QuantType.per_1x128
`use_g1u1`: 1
`doweight_stage1`: 0

## Technical Details

Five rows replaced in `aiter/configs/model_configs/a8w8_blockscale_tuned_fmoe_ds_v3.csv`:

| token | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| 2048 | `novs_silu_1tg_32x256`, bm 32, us 359.4797 | `vs_ps_silu_64x256`, bm 64, us 479.9735 |
| 4096 | `novs_silu_1tg_32x256`, bm 32, us 359.4797 | `vs_ps_silu_64x256`, bm 64, us 713.8348 |
| 8192 | `novs_silu_1tg_32x256`, bm 32, us 359.4797 | `vs_ps_silu_64x256`, bm 64, us 1342.4516 |
| 16384 | `novs_silu_1tg_ps_32x256`, bm 32, us 359.4797 | `vs_ps_silu_64x256`, bm 64, us 2399.3538 |
| 32768 | `novs_silu_1tg_ps_32x256`, bm 32, us 359.4797 | `vs_ps_silu_64x256`, bm 64, us 4747.7789 |

Rows for 1–1024 tokens are unchanged.

Tuned on MI355X (gfx950, cu_num 256), aiter 0.1.21.post2 with `csrc/ck_gemm_moe_2stages_codegen/gemm_moe_tune.py` against the keys in `aiter/configs/model_configs/a8w8_blockscale_untuned_fmoe_ds_v3.csv`.
