## 正文

### What this adds

One tuned table, `aiter/configs/model_configs/qwen3_8_2p4t_a95b_bf16_tuned_gemm.csv`, with **26 rows** covering five of the bf16 GEMM families Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B executes that AITER has no tuned entry for. Tuned on **gfx950, cu_num=256** (MI355X), TP8.

Learned from an earlier PR https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/5183, **no row uses hipBLASLt or opus.**  #5183 added 75 rows for the same model, of which 63 were hipBLASLt. Review asked for hipBLASLt to be dropped because its solution indices are library-build-specific. 

| N | K | rows | Module |
| ----- | ---- | ---- | --- |
| 8192 | 256 | 12 | MoE down-projection / GDN `out_proj` |
| 4608 | 8192 | 4 | Gated DeltaNet `in_proj_qkvz`, sharded TP8 |
| 512 | 8192 | 4 | MoE router gate |
| 32 | 8192 | 3 | Gated DeltaNet `in_proj_ba`, sharded TP8 |
| 31040 | 8192 | 3 | `lm_head`, vocab 248320 sharded TP8 |

The matching `qwen3_8_2p4t_a95b_bf16_untuned_gemm.csv` shape list is included so the table can be re-tuned without reconstructing the shapes by hand. It lists all **81** swept shapes across **seven** families

### Why these backends are safe to check in

| backend | rows | dispatched by | portable? |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `flydsl` | 19 | `kernelName` | yes — kernels live in AITER; falls through to default when a named kernel is absent from the build |
| `asm` | 4 | `kernelName` + `splitK` | yes — kernels live in AITER |
| `triton` | 3 | `solidx=0`, `kernelName=auto` | yes — nothing to index |

`hipblaslt` (`solidx` into hipBLASLt) and `opus` (`kernelId=int(solidx)` into opus) are both excluded.

### Every row beats the current fallback

Every row is required to beat, by at least 1.02x on the same build, so **no shape can regress relative to today's behavior**. Winners are the fastest candidate within **`err_ratio <= 0.005`**, not the fastest overall. The tuner's default `--errRatio 0.05` which we felt was too loose. Shapes where nothing portable won are deliberately absent so the default path is kept. 

| family | shapes swept | rows kept | best gain over fallback |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| N=8192, K=256 | 12 | 12 | **1.455x** at M=32 |
| N=512, K=8192 | 12 | 4 | **1.390x** at M=8 |
| N=4608, K=8192 | 12 | 4 | **1.223x** at M=16384 |
| N=32, K=8192 | 12 | 3 | **1.154x** at M=4096 |
| N=31040, K=8192 | 6 | 3 | **1.072x** at M=8 |
| N=1, K=8192 | 15 | 0 | nothing portable beats the fallback |
| N=513, K=8192 | 12 | 0 | nothing portable beats the fallback |


**23 of the 26 rows are exactly `err_ratio` 0.0.** The three that are not are the three split-k rows, at 0.0020, 0.0037 and 0.0048 — all inside the bound, but not bit-exact:

| M | N | K | libtype | splitK | err_ratio |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 32 | 8192 | flydsl | 2 | 0.0020 |
| 8 | 512 | 8192 | flydsl | 2 | 0.0037 |
| 16 | 512 | 8192 | flydsl | 2 | 0.0048 |


### Coverage

The lookup tries `gl in [None, 0, 1]`: exact M, then the two padded buckets from `get_padded_m`. Replaying that lookup against a production-shaped benchmark that logged **6,127 distinct (M, N, K)** shapes (ISL/OSL of 8k/1k, 60k/1k and 128k/1k, all one key variant — `torch.bfloat16` in and out, `bias=False`, `scaleAB=False`, `bpreshuffle=False`):

| N | K | observed shapes | served | served % |
| ----- | ---- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8192 | 256 | 1,182 | 1,182 | 100.0% |
| 32 | 8192 | 1,167 | 709 | 60.8% |
| 512 | 8192 | 1,179 | 709 | 60.1% |
| 31040 | 8192 | 232 | 99 | 42.7% |
| 4608 | 8192 | 1,182 | 50 | 4.2% |
| 1 | 8192 | 1,185 | 0 | 0.0% |
| **total** | | **6,127** | **2,749** | **44.9%** |

Tuning shapes were collected at `max_num_batched_tokens=16384` and `max_num_seqs=256`.

### End-to-end effect

Serving A/B under vLLM on `amd/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B-Quark-MXFP4`, TP8 on MI355X, 8k input / 1k output, arms differing only by this table.

**Geometric mean output throughput +5.1%, geometric mean mean-TTFT −9.6%, geometric mean mean-TPOT −4.6%.**
