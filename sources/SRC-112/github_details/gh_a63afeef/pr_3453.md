## 正文

Stacked on #3437 (base `cx-graphs-rank-major`). Retarget to `main` once that merges.

Graph-replays MoRI in both modes. It uses #3437's graph harness: rank-aligned replay, the chained pair period under capture, and the poisoned replay value check.

## Change
- **ROCm event path.** ROCm torch before 2.13 rejects `torch.cuda.Event(external=True)` ("External events are disallowed in rocm"), even though HIP ≥ 7 supports external event records inside a capture. The event is created normally, recorded once outside capture, and its in-capture record goes through `hipEventRecordWithFlags(..., hipEventRecordExternal)` via ctypes. That is the call torch 2.13 makes itself (pytorch#178264). CUDA backends are unchanged.
- **`ep_mori.py`: `CUDA_GRAPH_MODES = ("normal", "low-latency")`.** MoRI's kernels launch with host-built args only (the reset moved on-device in ROCm/mori#86 for vLLM's graphs), and MoRI's own benchmark captures IntraNode dispatch/combine.
- Unit tests for the HIP record path: 120 pass, 8 skipped.

## Validation
Two full sweeps of #3437 with this change included: [36189493665](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/actions/runs/36189493665), [36189496984](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/actions/runs/36189496984).

**Every MoRI cell passed every check in both runs:** mi355x and mi325x, normal and low-latency, EP8 and EP16, bf16 and fp8. That includes the poisoned graph-replay value check and the chained oracle.

MoRI low-latency on mi325x stays off: it fails **eager** on that pool (run [36189510167](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/actions/runs/36189510167)), independent of graphs. #3437 drops that leg.
