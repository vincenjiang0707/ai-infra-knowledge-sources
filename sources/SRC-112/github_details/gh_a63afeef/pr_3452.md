# [PR #3452] operatorx: gemm + moe on NVIDIA and AMD, measured in the serving framework's own layers

## 正文

OperatorX measures single ops (a GEMM, one MoE layer) on NVIDIA and AMD. It runs them through the same framework layers InferenceX serves with, so the kernel it times is the kernel real inference uses. This PR replaces the prototype under `experimental/operatorx/` with that version, adds the collection methodology, and connects the sweep to the OperatorX database.

## Scope
- **Ops: only `gemm` and `moe` for now.**
  - Attention, collectives, `moe_gemm`, `moe_forward`, `moe_layer`, the other NVIDIA backends, the TPU and Trainium runners, and the `scripts/inferencex_testlist` generator are removed. Ops and platforms will come back one at a time.
  - `gemm` (`ops/gemm.py`): activation `a` [M, K] and weight `b` [N, K]. Each is an operand descriptor `{"dtype", "scale"?, "scale2"?, "symmetric"?}`.
    - `scale` is `{"dtype", "static", "group": [rows, cols]}`, and `-1` spans a dimension.
    - So `[-1,-1]` is per-tensor, `[1,-1]` per-token, `[-1,1]` per-channel, `[1,g]` groups of g along K, and `[r,c]` 2-D blocks.
  - `moe` (`ops/moe.py`): one MoE layer from the router GEMM to the combined output. It has:
    - `tokens` and `hidden`;
    - `experts`: count, top-k, intermediate size, optional bias / latent / zero experts, and the operand descriptors `a1` (input), `w1` (fused gate/up weight), `w2` (down weight) and `a2` (intermediate activation), named after vLLM's `FusedMoEQuantConfig` slots;
    - `router`: gate dtype, scoring, selection, bias, renormalize, scale;
    - `activation`, optional `shared` experts, and a `routing` distribution (natural / balanced / zipf / single_hot).

    Kernels, dispatch, fusion and streams are the backend's choice.
  - **Every case says where it comes from:** `sources` lists one `<checkpoint>/<role>` per layer that runs it, e.g. `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Pro/attn.wq_b` or `zai-org/GLM-5-FP8/mlp`.
    - A shape shared by several models, or by several layers of one model, lists every pair, so each role stays tied to its model. The role is what follows the last `/`.
    - DeepSeek-V4 roles come from the checkpoints' own weight maps under vLLM's TP sharding and fusions. That covers the indexer, compressor, router, mHC and `head` (lm_head) shapes.
    - It's metadata and doesn't change what's measured.
- **Backends:**
  - `vllm` gemm (NVIDIA, AMD; `runners/common/vllm/linear.py`).
    - Builds vLLM's `ReplicatedLinear` under the quant config the descriptors imply, loads synthetic checkpoint-format weights, and runs `process_weights_after_loading`. vLLM then does its own activation quantization, repacking and kernel selection.
    - Emulation-only paths are reported unsupported. So is a quantized layer that comes out unquantized.
  - `vllm` moe (`runners/common/vllm/moe.py`): vLLM's `GateLinear` router, `FusedMoEFactory` experts and shared-expert MLP, wired as vLLM's model blocks wire them. Covers DeepSeek-V4 FP4 experts and hash routing, Kimi-K3 latent experts, Qwen's shared-expert gate, and forced expert loads.
  - `torch` (AMD): hipBLASLt bf16 and per-tensor fp8.
- **Testlists:**

  | Testlist | Cases | What |
  |---|---|---|
  | `gemm.json` | 5,416 | InferenceX matrix shapes under each checkpoint's per-layer quant scheme |
  | `gemm_serving_{8k1k,all}_min.json` | 1,505 / 8,099 | DeepSeek-V4-architecture shapes; `sources` is DSv4-Pro where the shape is one of its projections, else `[]` |
  | `gemm_perf.json` | 11 | BF16 smoke list |
  | `moe_small.json` | 62 | One full-size MoE layer per InferenceX MoE checkpoint scheme (32 checkpoints) at tokens=1; one layer per family swept at 8/64/512/2048 tokens; DeepSeek-R1 / Qwen3.5 FP8 under each expert-load distribution |

  gpt-oss is left out of `moe_small` for now. Its biased MXFP4 experts don't go through vLLM's mxfp4 method cleanly here yet.

## Measurement
- **GPU timing:**
  - Warmup has a wall-clock floor.
  - A GPU spin runs ahead of the timed loop, so launch overhead is excluded.
  - The cache is flushed before every iteration; the result is the median of 10.
- **Telemetry** (`runners/common/telemetry.py`):
  - NVML / amdsmi record clocks, power, temperatures and throttle reasons while each op runs.
  - Capped attempts are retried with growing inter-kernel sleeps.
- **Profiling** (`runners/common/profiling.py`, `runners/common/trace.py`):
  - A torch.profiler replay attaches a per-kernel breakdown (`metrics.profile`).
  - A platform-independent trace module (`trace.py`) with a small adapter per platform (CUDA/ROCm today).
- **Counters mode:** one marked replay per op under Nsight Compute or rocprofv3. Counter files go under `results/counters/`, and latencies are perturbed.

## CI (`operatorx-sweep.yml`)
- Inputs:
  - `runner`: a `cluster:` label (`cluster:h100-dgxc` default, then `h200-dgxc`, `b200-nscale`, `b300-dsxe`, `gb200-nv`, `gb300-nv`, `mi300x-amd`, `mi325x-amds`, `mi355x-amds`);
  - `backends`, `testlists`, `mode` (timing | counters), `world_sizes`, `chunk_size`.
- New last job `trigger-ingest`: sends InferenceX-app an `ingest-operatorx` repository_dispatch with the run id, as `run-sweep.yml` does for benchmark results. The app stores the run in the OperatorX database (SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX-app, branch `hbarclay/operatorx`). A run with failed shards is stored too; its unrun cases read as missing.
- Dispatched from `hbarclay/operatorx`:
  - `gemm` timing, 5,416 cases each: H200, B200, B300, MI300X, MI325X, MI355X.
  - `moe_small`: H200, B300, MI300X, MI325X, MI355X.
  - Counters mode: H200, B200, MI300X, MI325X, MI355X.
  - B300 counters needs Nsight Compute ≥ 2025.3 on the dsxe nodes.
  - MI355X `moe_small` reports a failure only because one shard is all-unsupported under `--strict`; its data is complete.
- `tests/test_ci.py` passes (32).
