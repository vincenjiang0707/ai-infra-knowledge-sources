## Summary

`triton.num_decompose_k_splits` now defaults to 10 on ROCm, the same as CUDA. The HIP special case that forced 0 splits, and the test-only `_DECOMPOSE_K_PATCH_ROCM` override that papered over it, are removed. The tests follow the default.

On MI350X (gfx950, 256 CUs, ROCm 7.2.2) a 474-matmul sweep with `max_autotune` and backends `ATEN,TRITON` does **not** support this as a performance default:

- Of 154 eligible shapes at cap 10, autotune picked decompose K 31 times, ATen 107 times, and the plain Triton `mm` template 16 times.
- Median decompose-K candidate was 22% slower than ATen. When both the Triton `mm` template and decompose K were timed, Triton `mm` won all 18 times.
- The large-K shapes this path is aimed at still pick ATen. 64×128×200000 and 64×64×177147 are 7–20% slower with decompose K.
- Offering the candidate costs about 1.1 s of compile time (p90 1.6 s). A 1024³ matmul, which is not eligible, shows no extra cost.
- Raising the cap from 10 to 20 added a new split on 2 shapes, and neither was more than 10% faster. gfx950 has no single best `k_split`; the winner is spread across 2–256 and differs for fp16 and bf16. Numerics matched eager on 112/154 eligible cap-10 results.

Draft until we decide the occasional small-tile win is worth the compile cost.
