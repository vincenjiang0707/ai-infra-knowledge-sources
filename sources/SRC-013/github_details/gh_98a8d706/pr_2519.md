## What does this PR do?

Adds an introductory AI infrastructure learning resource to the Getting Started page.

The page now includes an embedded video covering GPUs, vLLM, and llm-d, along with a direct YouTube link for users who prefer to watch on YouTube.

## Why is this change needed?

The Getting Started page is the natural entry point for users who are new to llm-d and distributed LLM inference. Providing an introductory learning resource helps readers understand the surrounding AI infrastructure concepts before moving on to deployment and Well-Lit Paths.

The video provides background on GPUs, vLLM, and llm-d, making the documentation more accessible to newcomers.
