## 正文

## What

Renames the `wide-ep-lws` guide to `wide-ep`. The guide deploys a DisaggregatedSet, so the lws substring is a leftover from when LeaderWorkerSets were the base.

Pure rename, 124 files, no functional change:

- `guides/wide-ep-lws/` -> `guides/wide-ep/`
- DS objects: `wide-ep-lws-nvidia-gpu-vllm*` -> `wide-ep-nvidia-gpu-vllm*`
- `llm-d.ai/guide` label, `wide-ep-epp` service, `router/wide-ep.values.yaml`
- The 10 nightly and consolidate-status workflows: filenames, `standup_scenario`, concurrency groups, namespaces, bucket paths, badge names
- All cross-references (agentic-serving, precise-prefix-cache-routing, docs, CONTRIBUTING)

Every kustomization in the guide (49) renders byte-identical to before modulo the name substitution, and the agentic-serving composition still builds.

Known side effects:

- Inbound links to `guides/wide-ep-lws/` (blog posts, external docs) break.
- The regression bucket path moves to `llm-d-benchmarks/regressions/wide-ep`, so nightly comparison history restarts under the new path.
