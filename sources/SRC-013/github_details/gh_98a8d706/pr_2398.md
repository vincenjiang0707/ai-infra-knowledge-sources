## 正文

Adds a variant guide under `guides/p2p-kv-cache-sharing/remaining-work-routing/`, linked from the parent P2P guide's variant sections.

The variant routes long-context follow-ups by their remaining prefill work instead of their total length: a request whose prefix is pullable over the P2P tier counts only its fresh tail, so it runs on a short-work prefill class and pulls the prefix instead of queueing behind cold long prefills. Prefill classes split by the `llm-d.ai/prefill-work-range` label; the boundary is derived from deployment measurements (peak per-rank prefill throughput plus the pull cost model) by the included `calibrate_work_boundary.py`, which also emits the labels and EPP parameters as one consistent set.

Contents: deploy manifests (GLM-5.2-FP8, 3x DP8 engines), router configuration for the baseline and remaining-work EPPs (one-parameter difference), the calibration script, the benchmark harness, and measured results on agentic traces (C64: migration-eligible TTFT p50 -53%, and -33% on top of an engine-side `--long-prefill-token-threshold` baseline).

Marked [Experimental]: the EPP side depends on llm-d-router#2559 (issue llm-d-router#2540), still in review, and the split-pool premise is scale-dependent - at two prefill engines an undivided pool with prefix-affinity scoring can outperform the split; the guide targets deployments that require work-class separation.

Relates to: llm-d-router#2540, llm-d-router#2559.
