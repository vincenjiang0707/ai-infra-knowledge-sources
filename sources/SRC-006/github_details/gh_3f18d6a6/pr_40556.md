## Motivation

#39421.

This PR moves DeepSelect to the existing JIT infrastructure so only the required call signature is compiled for the local CUDA target.

<!-- Describe the purpose and goals of this pull request. -->

## Modifications

- Remove the DeepSelect AOT target, bindings, and explicit instantiations.
- Move the vendored kernel headers under `jit/csrc/deepselect/vendor`.
- Add a JIT C++ entry point and Python wrapper for BF16/FP32, int32/int64
  indices, normal and cluster kernels, sorting, offsets, and variable lengths.
- Keep the DeepSelect CUDA algorithm unchanged. The three kernel headers only
  drop includes of the deleted AOT `topk_select.h` files.
- Add C++ argument/storage validation, aligned output staging, and empty-batch
  handling.
- Use the JIT framework's local target directly. No `ScopedCudaDevice`, runtime
  C++ SM check, or `override_jit_cuda_arch` is added.

## Speed Tests and Profiling


The complete candidate path was benchmarked on NVIDIA H20 using:

- FP32 candidate logits.
- `topk_blocks=2048`.
- 20 warmup iterations.
- 100 measured iterations.
- Median latency.
- L2 cache flushing before every measured iteration.
- Two complete benchmark runs; the tables report the mean of the two medians.

The measured region includes:

```text
candidate block-score reduction
  -> candidate Top-K
  -> candidate-mask publication
  -> downstream paged Top-K
```

### Final Top-K 2048

| Rows | Sequence length | PyTorch | Fused AOT | Speedup |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 128K | 154.304 us | 45.056 us | 3.42x |
| 1 | 256K | 176.864 us | 64.720 us | 2.73x |
| 1 | 512K | 179.872 us | 75.168 us | 2.39x |
| 1 | 1M | 187.600 us | 98.096 us | 1.91x |
| 6 | 128K | 167.328 us | 45.168 us | 3.70x |
| 6 | 256K | 180.592 us | 60.656 us | 2.98x |
| 6 | 512K | 204.368 us | 74.256 us | 2.75x |
| 6 | 1M | 279.296 us | 106.032 us | 2.63x |

Across all 16 shapes:

- Speedup range: `1.91x–3.77x`.
- Geometric-mean speedup: `2.79x`.
- Best result: `3.77x` for 6 rows, 128K sequence length, and final Top-K 512.

The AOT Top-K kernel was also compared directly with the external DeepSelect implementation at width 131072 and Top-K 2048. The latency difference was approximately 0.3% to 0.4%, indicating that moving the kernel into `sgl-kernel` does not introduce a kernel-level performance regression.
