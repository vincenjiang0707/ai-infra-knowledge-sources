## 正文

## Motivation

This is a ROCm performance change for gfx950 fp8 hd256 chunked prefill: at `--page-size 64` it hands aiter's paged-varlen ASM kernel ([ROCm/aiter#4971](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/4971)) a page-level view of the KV pool, replacing the gather that main (#36758) runs on every layer to copy the prefix KV into a contiguous buffer, and is worth **+1.1% to +1.9% output tok/s at 60k/600 and +0.9% to +1.6% at 128k/1k**, with TTFT 2% to 7% better, for `amd/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B-Quark-MXFP4` on 8x MI355X, TP8, fp8 KV, measured on two nodes against the released `lmsysorg/sglang:v0.5.19-rocm10-mi35x` image at its default `--page-size 1`.

The gather exists because without a page-level table aiter reads a page-64 call as `page_size=1` and this shape falls to CK. It works, but it pays for a copy and a scratch buffer sized by the total KV in the forward, not by the chunk. Reading the pool in place removes both. Per prefill call that is 1.04x to 1.22x faster than the gather (2.8x to 3.7x over CK), and the gap grows with context length and with the number of requests prefilling together. The gather's scratch peaks at 451 MB in the largest case measured, eight prefills over a 111k-token prefix; this path allocates none. At 8k/1k the end-to-end result is flat, -0.1% to +0.9% throughput.

The change is contained. It only engages on gfx95 at `--page-size 64` with fp8 KV, head dim 256, a power-of-two GQA ratio and the plain NHD pool (not the sliding-window or vectorized 5D pool). Everywhere else prefill runs exactly as on main, and `SGLANG_AITER_PAGED_PREFILL_ASM=0` restores main's behaviour even there. Page 64 is the only page size aiter's kernel is compiled for, so at `--page-size 16`, where #37465 puts the MTP ASM verify kernel, this path is a no-op. The two cannot both be active on one server until one of them supports the other's page size.

main's `docker/rocm.Dockerfile` pins aiter `acf8fdf` (2026-09-12), which already contains aiter#4971, so no aiter bump is needed.

## Modifications

**`python/sglang/srt/layers/attention/aiter_backend.py`**

- `_build_paged_kv_view()` converts the token-level KV table into `(page_indptr, page_ids, last_page_len)`. The allocator packs each page contiguously, so a slot's page id is `slot // page_size`; the arithmetic runs off `seq_lens_cpu`, so only the gather touches the GPU.
- `ForwardMetadata.paged_kv_view` carries it. It is built once per batch in `init_forward_metadata`, and only where the whole batch can use it: gfx95, page 64, and the plain NHD pool. Off gfx95 there is no kernel for this shape, so the view is never built rather than built and discarded.
- `forward_extend` computes `paged_asm_available` once per layer (view present and power-of-two GQA ratio via `_paged_prefill_asm_supports_gqa()`). The #36758 gather branch requires it to be `False`; the paged branch requires it to be `True`, and then swaps in the 4D K/V views, the page-level indptr/table and `kv_last_page_lens`. One flag read at both sites keeps the two branches mutually exclusive. The guard mirrors aiter's own: there is no CK arm for 4D LINEAR page-64 fp8 hd256, so a call aiter rejects raises `no matching kernel found` instead of falling back.
- `SGLANG_AITER_PAGED_PREFILL_ASM` (default on) gates whether the view is built at all.

**`python/sglang/srt/environ.py`** - `SGLANG_AITER_PAGED_PREFILL_ASM = EnvBool(True)`.

**`docs/docs/references/environment_variables.mdx`** - documents the flag, including that it is inert at any page size other than 64.

**`test/manual/test_aiter_paged_kv_view.py`** - CPU-only. `TestAiterPagedKVView` checks the conversion against a shuffled allocation with a ragged last page, since an identity table would pass even if the arithmetic were wrong; `TestPagedPrefillAsmSupportsGqa` covers the GQA guard, including indivisible and zero/negative head counts.

The flag is a plain on/off rather than a context-length heuristic. The paged path wins at 60k and above and is flat at 8k, but the crossover sits inside this host's noise, so a threshold picked from this data would not be defensible. Default on because the long-context win is the larger effect.

This host shows reproducible whole-server stalls: the scheduler stops logging and no requests complete for 13-18 s, roughly every 2-4 minutes, with occasional 30-55 s events. 60k/16 is where they hit hardest, so it was run three times per arm. main: 155.4, 156.7, 157.1, none carrying a stall. ### Op-level: why not just gather

Both paths reach the same ASM family, so the choice is measurable on one prefill call. aiter with #4971, page 64, hd256, GQA 8:1, causal, medians of three runs. Wall clock per call, so the gather's copy and index arithmetic are inside the timed region. The budget is held at 16384 new tokens, what `--chunked-prefill-size` caps, and split across the batch:

| prefilling reqs | chunk each | KV prefix | CK | gather + contiguous asm | page view + paged asm | ratio | gather scratch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 16384 | 0 | 1544 us | 570 us | **549 us** | 1.04x | +8 MB |
| 1 | 16384 | 43,616 | 9213 us | 2892 us | **2595 us** | 1.11x | +30 MB |
| 1 | 16384 | 111,616 | 21310 us | 6577 us | **5685 us** | 1.16x | +64 MB |
| 4 | 4096 | 0 | 432 us | 230 us | **203 us** | 1.14x | +8 MB |
| 4 | 4096 | 43,616 | 8185 us | 2673 us | **2355 us** | 1.14x | +95 MB |
| 4 | 4096 | 111,616 | 20354 us | 6527 us | **5523 us** | 1.18x | +230 MB |
| 8 | 2048 | 0 | 251 us | 176 us | **150 us** | 1.18x | +8 MB |
| 8 | 2048 | 43,616 | 8019 us | 2720 us | **2334 us** | 1.17x | +181 MB |
| 8 | 2048 | 111,616 | 22373 us | 6859 us | **5602 us** | 1.22x | +451 MB |

The scratch is the gathered K/V buffer, `2 * total_k * H_kv * D`, allocated and freed per layer, so the column is the transient peak. This path allocates none of it; both paths need the same 128 MB for q and the output.

<details>
<summary>Where the time goes, and two controls</summary>

Timed separately, the index arithmetic costs 74-152 us and the copy 102-514 us, so together they cover about a fifth of the 892 us gap at one request and about half of the 1257 us gap at eight. The rest is the kernel: the contiguous ASM kernel on a gathered buffer is slower on this shape than the paged ASM kernel reading the pool in place, so even with a free gather this path stays ahead. Those splits are upper bounds, since measuring them adds device syncs.

A device-side kernel-time measurement would show none of this - the copy runs as separate `index_select` kernels before the attention call and the index arithmetic is host-side - so wall clock per call is the framing that charges each path for what it does.

Controls: the gather is page-size-insensitive at long context, page 64 landing within 0.3% of page 1 at both 43k and 111k prefix, as it should since `kv_indices` is token-level at either page size; at no prefix page 64 is 8-12% faster, where the scattered page-1 allocation costs the copy its locality. The batch-1 cells reproduce within 4% on a second node.

</details>
