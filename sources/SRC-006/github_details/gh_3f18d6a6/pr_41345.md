# [PR #41345] [DSV4.1][HiCache] fix: wait for the layer transfer before reading low-ratio index-K

source: https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/41345
state: open | updated: 2026-09-27T03:20:54Z
labels: deepseek, run-ci, run-ci-extra, memory-pool, bypass-fail-fast

## 正文

## Motivation

`DeepSeekV4TokenToKVPool.get_low_ratio_index_k_dequant` and `get_low_ratio_index_k_fp4` read index-K rows without `wait_layer_transfer(layer_id)`, while every other getter in the pool waits first. Their callers are the V4.1 low-ratio indexer paths (`deepseek_v4_backend.py`: dense fp4 prefill on SM100, and the SM90 torch path), and nothing earlier in those layers waits. With HiCache L2 load-back, the indexer can therefore read index-K before the host-to-device copy of that layer lands and pick top-k from stale rows (silent quality loss, no crash).

This reproduced on V4.1-Flash TP4 with HiCache L2: re-reading the same rows after `wait_layer_transfer` showed up to 98% of the rows changed at a late layer once the per-layer load window was widened.

## Modifications

Wait for the layer's transfer at the top of both getters, like the sibling getters.

## Accuracy Tests

`test_indexer_access_uses_layer_ratio_and_waits_only_for_reads` now also covers the two low-ratio getters and checks the wait precedes each read. It fails without this change and passes with it; `test_dsv4_compressed_pools.py`: 9 passed.
