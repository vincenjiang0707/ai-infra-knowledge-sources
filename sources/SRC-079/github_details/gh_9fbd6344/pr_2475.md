## 正文

### What does this PR do?

Type of change: documentation.

Chad's Agent: replace the 1% truncation-only rule with an inclusive, unrounded 2% aggregate model-output-fault tolerance per benchmark/run. The numerator is the deduplicated union of unique successful model responses unusable because they are length/token-budget truncated, have an empty final, or have a malformed/unusable final. A qualifying response must be preserved and scored incorrect; overlapping categories count once.

The change follows repeated complete Kimi SciCode runs that were excluded solely for one or two length-capped empty finals (under 1%), causing many unnecessary reruns. Attempt 15 completed 65/65 samples and scored 338/338 trajectories. It had 337 unique successful HTTP model responses, one preserved response that was both length-capped and empty-final, no HTTP or infrastructure errors, and score 53.25443786982248. Its deduplicated fault rate was 1/337 = 0.2967%. The new policy accepts that run with a visible warning and no rerun.

The tolerance does not waive missing or unscored responses, incomplete coverage, exhausted HTTP/transport/server/auth failures, infrastructure or secret failures, parser fallbacks/exceptions, harness/sandbox/scorer failures, wrong benchmark/model/config/version, protocol errors, or unknown denominators. Above 2%, return findings and a recommendation without automatically retrying or declaring success. Never tune or remove token limits merely to pass.

Parent guidance and both evaluator definitions share the policy. Vendored skills remain untouched; canonical run-validation guidance supersedes conflicting advice. Independent MLflow verification/export gates remain unchanged.
