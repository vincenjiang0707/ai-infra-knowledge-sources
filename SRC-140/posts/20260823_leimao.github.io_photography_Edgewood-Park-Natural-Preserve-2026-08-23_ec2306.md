# Edgewood Park Natural Preserve

Introduction

On August 23rd, 2026, I visited Edgewood Park Natural Preserve at Redwood City, California, for a short hike and photography. I did not encounter too many creatures but a few birds near the [Mountain Top Viewpoint](https://maps.app.goo.gl/cWbsJKvrAuvGRLdQ9), including American Bushtit, Steller’s Jay, and California Scrub-Jay.

Camera System Setup

- Camera: Sony Alpha 1 II
- Lens: Tamron 50-400mm F/4.5-6.3 Di III VC VXD
