# Vasona Lake County Park

Introduction

On September 12th, 2026, I took some photographs at Vasona Lake County Park at Los Gatos after I completed the [2026 LSA’s Run Home 10K](https://leimao.github.io/life/2026-LSA-Run-Home-10K/) race in the morning. There are a variety of waterfowls living in the Vasona Lake, including American Coots, Pied-Billed Grebes, and Green Herons. It is also a good place for fishing. I could see Largemouth Basses, Western Pond Turtles, and American Bullfrogs near the lakeshore. This is the first I photographed with Sony FE 100-400mm F4.5 GM OSS lens. APSC crop mode was used frequently in many situations where extra reach was needed.

Camera System Setup

- Camera: Sony Alpha 1 II
- Lens: Sony FE 100-400mm F4.5 GM OSS
