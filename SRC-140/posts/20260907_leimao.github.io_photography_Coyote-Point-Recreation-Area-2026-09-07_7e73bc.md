# Coyote Point Recreation Area

Introduction

On September 7th, 2026, I visited [Coyote Point Recreation Area](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/coyote-point-recreation-area) for hiking and photography. Although there are not too many wildlife and birds in this area, it is a great place to photograph airplanes that are about to land at San Francisco International Airport. In addition, it is also a popular place for swimming and fishing.
